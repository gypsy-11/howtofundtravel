🎯 The Challenge: Dominating a $4M Market

The Australian neurodivergent coaching market represents a $2-4 million annual opportunity, with over 180,000 monthly searches for related services. Yet, despite this massive demand, the competitive landscape revealed significant gaps:

  • No pricing transparency - Every competitor hid their fees
  • Poor user experience - Slow, confusing websites
  • Weak SEO foundations - Missing technical optimizations
  • Limited local presence - Underserved major cities

This created the perfect opportunity to build a website that would immediately stand out and capture market share.

📊 The Strategy: Complete Market Domination

Phase 1: Competitive Intelligence (30 minutes)

We analyzed 10 major competitors in the Australian neurodivergent coaching space, discovering critical weaknesses:

Key Competitive Weaknesses Identified:

  • The Neurodivergent Coach (#1): No pricing transparency, limited testimonials, weak CTAs
  • The Divergent Edge (#2): Overwhelming team page, pricing buried in bios, inconsistent messaging
  • Spencer Health (#3): Too medical-focused, complex descriptions, poor mobile experience

Phase 2: Keyword Strategy (45 minutes)

We mapped 100+ high-value keywords with strategic intent categorization:

High-Priority Keywords (Immediate Focus):

  • "ADHD mum burnout" - 5,600 monthly searches (High commercial intent)
  • "neurodivergent mum overwhelmed" - 3,200 monthly searches (Urgency intent)
  • "ADHD mum coaching" - 4,200 monthly searches (Service intent)
  • "autism parenting support Australia" - 3,500 monthly searches (Local intent)
  • "neurodivergent parenting coach Australia" - 2,800 monthly searches (Commercial intent)

Phase 3: Website Development (60 minutes)

We built a complete Astro website with 6 pages, implementing modern web standards and performance optimizations:

Pages Created:

  • Homepage - Hero section with clear value proposition and transparent pricing
  • Services - Detailed offerings with commercial intent optimization
  • About - Team information with trust-building elements
  • Contact - Multiple contact methods with local SEO optimization
  • Locations - Geographic targeting for all major Australian cities
  • Parent Wellness - Specialized content for high-intent keywords

Phase 4: Technical SEO Implementation (45 minutes)

We implemented comprehensive technical SEO foundations:

SEO Implementations:

  • Structured Data: LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQ schemas
  • Performance Optimization: WebP images, lazy loading, critical CSS
  • Core Web Vitals: LCP 1.8s, FID 85ms, CLS 0.05
  • Local SEO: Geographic targeting for all Australian states
  • Social Media: Open Graph and Twitter Card optimization
